
# Water-bottle
The objective of this project is to develop a conceptual smart vacuum flask as a simulated commercial product by applying the engineering knowledge and skills acquired throughout the semester.The project aims to integrate product design, material selection, electronic components, manufacturing processes, cost estimation, packaging, and marketing considerations to develop a product that is technically feasible and commercially viable. Through this project, the feasibility of designing and introducing the product to the market will be evaluated while demonstrating the practical application of the knowledge learned during the course.
